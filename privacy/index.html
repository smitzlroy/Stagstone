Privacy notice

Discretion is central to what we do. This notice explains how Stagstone Protection Ltd (“we”, “us”) handles personal information you share with us through this website or when you contact us.

Who we are

Stagstone Protection Ltd is the data controller for information collected through this website. You can contact us at scott@stagstoneprotection.com.

What we collect

  • Details you provide when you contact us: your name, email address, telephone number and the content of your enquiry.
  • Any further information you choose to share with us during subsequent discussions.

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to your enquiry, to discuss and provide our services, and to keep appropriate records. Our lawful basis is your consent and our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries, and — where you become a client — the performance of a contract.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell or share your information for marketing. We only share it where necessary to deliver services you have asked for, or where required by law. We keep enquiry information for no longer than necessary and delete it when it is no longer needed.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to access, correct or delete your information, or object to how it is used. Contact us using the details above. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).
