Our founder’s experience

Royal protection experience

Our approach draws on senior experience supporting Royal security in Scotland and serving at Royal residences — an understanding of the discretion, preparation and attention to detail that high-profile settings require.

Why it matters

Standards learned where discretion is everything.

Protecting high-profile people in historic, working environments demands more than vigilance. It calls for quiet professionalism, careful preparation and the ability to work seamlessly alongside household, estate and visiting teams.

Before founding Stagstone Protection, Scott Cox held senior Royal and heritage security roles in Scotland, including at Balmoral Castle and the Palace of Holyroodhouse, with senior responsibility for Royal security in Scotland. Working closely with estate leadership, he contributed to protective planning and site operations.

Those same standards now shape how we support private clients, their families and their homes.
