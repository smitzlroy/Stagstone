Residential Security Teams

Protecting the household, quietly.

Planning and coordination of residential security arrangements that protect the household while allowing daily life to continue with as little disruption as possible.

Drawing on experience advising on protective staffing and security measures at UHNW properties — and 12 months supporting a UHNW family’s Residential Security Team — we help design teams that are the right size, well briefed and properly integrated with the household.

  • Review of the residence, routines and risk profile
  • Advice on team size, structure, roles and shift patterns
  • Standing orders, post instructions and reporting lines
  • Integration with household staff, drivers and estate teams
  • Access control, visitor and delivery procedures
  • Incident response and escalation planning
  • Ongoing oversight, quality checks and reviews

The aim is reassurance — security the family can rely on, without it defining their home.

Close Protection Officers

Close protection, calmly delivered.

Professional close protection support tailored to the client’s movements, schedule and risk environment, with an emphasis on discretion, preparation and clear communication.

Good close protection is mostly unseen: thorough advance work, sensible routes and timings, and officers who understand both the principal’s needs and the setting they are working in.

  • Assessment of the itinerary and risk environment
  • Selection and coordination of suitable CPOs
  • Advance planning for venues, routes and travel
  • Liaison with drivers, residences, hosts and venues
  • Clear communication with the principal and their office
  • Low-profile, proportionate presence
